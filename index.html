/ Applications

Applications & Industries

Hydraulic breakers turn your excavator or backhoe into a precision breaking tool. Here's where the DS series earns its keep.

Mining

In mining, breakers handle secondary breaking of oversize boulders that can't pass crushers or grizzlies, and selective breaking around ore bodies. Heavy DS models (DS81–DS131) suit 18–40 ton excavators working hard rock faces day after day.

Quarrying

Quarries use breakers for oversize reduction, bench and face work, and breaking out dimension stone with control. The mid and heavy DS range matches the carrier sizes most common on quarry fleets.

Construction & excavation

Foundation excavation, trenching through rock, footing work and site preparation. Compact DS20–DS43 models pair with small excavators and backhoe loaders for tight urban sites.

Demolition

Breakers demolish reinforced concrete, foundations, bridge decks and road slabs with controlled impact — faster and more precisely than wrecking methods, with the carrier you already own.

Road construction & infrastructure

Hill cutting, road widening, canal and pipeline trenches, and foundation work for bridges and structures. With models spanning 1–40 ton carriers, one DS range covers an entire project fleet.

Choosing the right breaker

1 · Match the carrier

Start with your excavator or backhoe tonnage. Each DS model's applicable excavator range is in the spec table — never exceed your carrier's lifting and hydraulic capacity.

2 · Match the hydraulics

Check your machine's oil flow (l/min) and operating pressure against the model's optimum ranges. Running outside them costs impact power and breaker life.

3 · Match the material

Harder, more abrasive rock and high daily hours favour a heavier class with a larger rod diameter. Use the finder on the Products page, or ask us.

Frequently asked questions

Which Driezstone model fits my excavator?
Match your carrier tonnage to the "Applicable Excavator" row in the spec table: DS20 suits 1–3 t, DS30 3–5 t, DS40 5–7 t, DS43 7–10 t, DS50 10–16 t, DS81 18–24 t, DS121 25–33 t and DS131 34–40 t. Or use the finder on the Products page.
